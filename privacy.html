Privacy Policy

Fractal Atlas · Last updated September 26, 2026

Fractal Atlas does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no network requests from the app to any server.

What the app does on your device

All fractal rendering happens locally on your device's GPU. Nothing about how you use the app — what you view, zoom into, or how often you open it — ever leaves your device.

Sharing a view

If you choose to save an image, it is saved directly to your photo library. If you choose to share an image or a link, that uses iOS's built-in share sheet. Both are entirely your choice; the app does not automatically send anything anywhere.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, or contacts. If you choose to save an image, iOS will ask for permission to add photos to your library; the app can only add images, never read or view your existing photos.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first, before that change ships in an update.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
